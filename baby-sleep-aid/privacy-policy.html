Baby Sleep Aid – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Baby Sleep Aid is an app that plays soothing sounds to help babies fall asleep. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Summary

  • The app itself has no accounts and does not ask you for personal information such as your name or email.
  • The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, AdMob collects some device information described below.
  • Your “My Lullaby” voice recording is never sent off your phone.
  • Your sleep log is stored only on your phone and is never sent to the developer or to advertising services.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows Google AdMob ads at the bottom of the screen. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Advertising ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • your interactions with ads (for example views and taps);
  • device model, operating system version and crash/diagnostic information.

How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google’s consent form for personalized ads when the app is first opened. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Microphone and the “My Lullaby” recording

With the “My Lullaby” feature you can record a lullaby in your own voice. Microphone permission is requested only when you tap the record button, and the microphone is used only while recording. The recording:

  • is stored only on your phone, in the app’s private folder that other apps cannot access;
  • is never uploaded to any server or shared with anyone, including advertising services;
  • is excluded from Google Drive cloud backup (it may only move to a new phone when you transfer your data directly by cable or from a nearby device);
  • is deleted completely with the “Delete” button in the app or by uninstalling the app.

Sleep log

The app keeps a sleep log so you can track your baby’s sleep. When you start a sound, the start time of the sleep is recorded; when you tap “Woke up”, the end time is recorded. You can also add or edit entries manually. The log contains only the start and end times of sleeps. The log:

  • is stored only on your phone, in the app’s private folder that other apps cannot access;
  • is never uploaded to any server or shared with anyone, including advertising services;
  • may be included in Android’s own backup (Google Drive) if Android backup is turned on, so that it can move to a new phone; the developer has no access to that backup;
  • can be deleted entry by entry with “Delete sleep” in the app, or completely by uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s data in Android Settings.

Notifications

Notification permission is used to show play, pause and stop controls on the lock screen and in the notification shade while a sound is playing.

App settings

Your selected sound, duration, volume and language are stored only on your phone. If Android backup is turned on, these settings may be included in Android’s own backup; the developer has no access to that backup.

Children’s privacy

The app is designed for parents and is not directed at children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the date above will be updated.
