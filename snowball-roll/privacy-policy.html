Snowball Roll – Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

DoDe Games develops Snowball Roll. You can play offline without an account. The game is ad-free and does not use in-game analytics or track you across apps or websites.

Game data stored on your device

Snowball Roll uses local device storage to remember:

  • Your best distance and in-game crystal balance.
  • The snowball skins you have unlocked and the skin you selected.
  • Your language, sound and graphics settings, and whether you have seen the controls hint.

The game uses these records to restore your progress and preferences. It does not send them to the developer or upload them to a game server.

Retention and deletion

Local records stay on your device until you delete the app and its data. If your device's backup settings include app data, a backup may keep a copy and restore it later. Manage those copies in your device or backup settings. The developer has no access to your device backups.

Opening this policy

The Privacy Policy button in Settings opens this page in your browser. GitHub Pages hosts the page and logs visitors' IP addresses for security. Its handling of that information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact and privacy requests

For questions or requests about your information, email DoDe Games at dooogukan@gmail.com. If you email the developer, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to respond to your request. Correspondence is kept as long as needed to handle the request or comply with legal obligations.
